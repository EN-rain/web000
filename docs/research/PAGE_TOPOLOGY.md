# Silver Palace page topology

## `/en-us/home`

1. Fixed translucent black top bar.
2. Full-viewport layered 2667x1440 parallax artwork.
3. Bottom character markers and vignette are part of the layered artwork.
4. Pre-registration ticket floats near the lower-right on desktop and lower-center on mobile.
5. The landing viewport does not vertically scroll.

Interaction model: pointer-driven parallax on desktop, static crop on mobile, click-driven navigation.

## `/en-us/world`

1. Fixed translucent black top bar.
2. Full-viewport dark panoramic city background.
3. Central stack of four oversized paper sheets.
4. Fixed right-hand thumbnail navigation on desktop.
5. Active paper can be vertically panned inside the viewport.

Interaction model: click-driven paper switching, hover-driven paper overlays, drag/wheel panning.
